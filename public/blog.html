Practice Update • Charlotte, NC

Practicing in Our New Home in Charlotte, NC!

Just moved to Charlotte, NC and looking forward to practicing in my new home!

After serving wonderful clients in Georgia and serving on the board of the Georgia Reflexology Organization (GRO), we are thrilled to be settled in Charlotte, North Carolina. I am proud to be a member of the North Carolina Reflexology Association (NCRA) alongside my national certification through the American Reflexology Certification Board (ARCB) and membership in the Reflexology Association of America (RAA).

In addition to private foot and hand reflexology sessions (including Western, Thai Foot Reflexology, and Zollinger Method Aromatherapy), I am honored to share reflexology within the Charlotte community—including one-on-one restorative foot reflexology sessions with Carolina Breast Friends at The Pink House and local dance and wellness events.

To schedule an appointment or ask a question about how reflexology can support your health journey, call or text 678-801-6064 or email jean@wonderfully-made.org.
