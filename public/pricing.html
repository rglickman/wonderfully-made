What to Expect at Your Session

Everything you need to know before your foot or hand reflexology appointment with Jean.

1. Comfortable & Non-Invasive

You remain fully clothed during your session—simply removing your shoes and socks for a foot reflexology session. You will relax comfortably while Jean works the reflex zones of your feet or hands.

2. Tailored to Your Preferences

Whether you prefer traditional Western reflexology, Thai foot reflexology, or the Zollinger method enhanced with soothing essential oils (or completely unscented without oils), your session is customized to you.

3. Post-Session Hydration & Rest

Because reflexology stimulates circulation, lymphatic flow, and natural detoxification processes, drinking plenty of water after your session helps your body flush toxins and enjoy lasting relaxation.
